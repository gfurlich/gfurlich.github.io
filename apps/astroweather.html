Astronomer's Observing Conditions Report

Hover over the chart to read values for any hour. Darker = better observing conditions on every row.

About this report

  • Reading the chart: each square is one hour in local time. Hover or tap an hour to see its values in the right-hand column.
  • Cloud Cover: forecast total cloud cover.
  • Transparency: estimated extra extinction at the zenith, in magnitudes, from aerosol optical depth, high cloud and humid haze.
  • Seeing: estimated image blur (FWHM) in arcseconds, from the forecast temperature and wind profile: turbulence above 1 km plus a surface-wind ground-layer term. It is a relative guide, not a calibrated measurement.
  • Darkness: sun altitude (day, civil, nautical and astronomical twilight, night), brightened by moonlight.
  • Ground Wind is at 10 m; Upper Wind is at 250 hPa (~10 km).
  • Overall Score = clear sky × mean(transparency, seeing) × night-sky darkness (sun and moon), shown 0–100.

Weather data: Open-Meteo. Aerosol data: CAMS (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service) via Open-Meteo.
